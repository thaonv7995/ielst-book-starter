Over the years, I've interviewed hundreds of candidates for
jobs at many different levels. The point of every job interview
is to make sure a candidate has the skills necessary to do the
work, Hiring the wrong person can be an expensive mistake.
But, apart from references, how can you determine if the
candidate actually knows what he says he knows? A very
effective way to sort out the good candidates from the bad i
by asking ‘How did you do that?’ and ‘Why did you do that?”
at appropriate stages in the interview.
