2 Lexical Resource - avoiding repetition

To achieve a high band score in the IELTS Writing paper, you need to
show a wide variety of vocabulary and sentence structure.
You can avoid repeating words by

* using asynonym
* using a pronoun to refer back to an idea (This + noun / These +
plural noun)

* using a group noun (This/these type(s) of / kind(s) of + noun)
